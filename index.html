TrackPoint technique

Rest your hands on the keyboard. Push the red cap gently in the direction you want the pointer to move. Use the physical buttons below the spacebar to click. Relax between movements.

Tip: if the pointer feels too fast or too slow, adjust TrackPoint sensitivity in your operating system settings.
